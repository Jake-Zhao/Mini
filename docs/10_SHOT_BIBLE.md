# 10 · Shot Bible

## Frame
- Orientation: landscape only
- Reference aspect: 1774 × 887 (~2.0:1)
- Primary focal subject: speed readout
- Speed visible-outline center: screen center ± 2% width/height
- Gear: lower-right of hero digits, visually subordinate
- Companion vehicle: right-lower road lane, background narrative only

## Camera
The camera is fixed to the virtual driver viewpoint. Avoid camera shake.

Starting targets (tune only against reference):
- FOV horizontal: 58–66 degrees
- camera height: 1.15–1.35 m
- pitch: 0 to -2 degrees
- roll: 0 degrees
- horizon: ~58–64% screen height
- vanishing point: centered horizontally

## Lens behavior
- No fake zoom pulse on speed changes.
- Mild breathing is allowed only through background exposure / atmospheric density, not by scaling HUD.
- Depth of field is subtle; hero HUD is never in DoF.

## Vehicle staging
- Vehicle appears roughly one lane to the right and slightly ahead.
- The vehicle should feel speed-matched: its screen position is mostly stable while the world moves.
- Ground contact must be obvious through shadow, reflection footprint and spray origin.
- Vehicle may not become a second focal subject.

## Light design
Primary palette:
- near-black green environment
- emerald practicals
- small controlled red accents
- white / silver hero speed

Rules:
- brightest large object = hero speed
- brightest environmental points = practical lamps / wet glints
- binary rain always below hero brightness
- red is a scarce accent, not a fill color

## Road
The road provides most of the speed sensation.
- near road flows fastest
- vanishing point remains comparatively stable
- reflections break and smear with roughness and wetness
- avoid mirror-like perfect symmetry

## Binary rain
- occupies upper half and fades naturally into road atmosphere
- content source is UTF-8 binary of `一路平安`
- multiple depth layers, not a single flat curtain
- darkness and depth are more important than legibility
